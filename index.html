    FAQ

    Good to know.

    What do you need from me to get started?

    A short brief: what the video is for, who it's for, where it will be posted, and any references you like. If you have raw footage, send it over. If not, we plan a shoot.

    Can you edit footage I've already shot?

    Yes. Send your raw clips and I'll handle the edit, colour, sound and graphics.

    How long does a project take?

    A short reel is much quicker than a brand film. Tell me your deadline and I'll tell you honestly what's realistic.

    How many revisions are included?

    Revision rounds are agreed upfront so there are no surprises.

    What formats will I receive?

    Horizontal for YouTube, vertical for Reels and Shorts, plus any other cut-downs you need.
